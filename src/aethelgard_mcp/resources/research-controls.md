# Research audit and valuation controls

Reconcile each forward operating and investment assumption with dated issuer guidance. A lower future capex ratio than current guidance requires an economic explanation and a persistent-guidance sensitivity. Treat integrated cash schedules as conditional models: mathematical reconciliation does not validate demand, replacement costs, acquisition returns or a proposed refinancing.

Apply these controls when financial research, valuation, market-event interpretation or portfolio comparisons depend on multiple sources or model outputs. Scale the work to the decision. These are reusable research instructions, not a trained model, measured accuracy improvement or continuing monitoring service.

## Chronology, source version and entity identity

Record event date, effective date, first verified publication time, update time, observation period and retrieval time separately, with timezone. A later retrieval or HTTP modification time is evidence about a source version, not proof that every statement was known at an earlier cutoff. Freeze source excerpts, filing accession, dated copies and input hashes where practical. Label indexed-only or inaccessible bodies; do not upgrade them into fully read primary evidence.

When quarterly filings cannot answer a recent event, search current reporting, open and read the relevant articles, then follow named primary sources. Vary legal entity names, dates, fiscal periods and terminology when the first query fails. Do not stop at a generic disclosure-lag explanation when news or direct statements may resolve the question. Record unsuccessful searches and distinguish article summaries from bodies actually read.

Resolve the legal entity and security using stable identifiers. Distinguish related managers, common shares, options and indirect fund exposures. A quarter-end holdings change does not identify transaction day, execution price, motive or current net exposure. Inspect relevant new ETF positions before describing an exit from an underlying company or sector. Use contemporaneous holdings weights for a dated look-through calculation; do not combine different factsheet vintages into false precision.

Distinguish a credit rating from its outlook, a credit opinion from an equity recommendation, an inquiry from an adjudicated violation, and announcement from completion. Verify causal chronology. Evidence that news was distributed after a market close does not establish when concern first became public or what caused the earlier price move. Preserve material qualifications and opposing mechanisms from the full source.

## One comparison contract

Before comparing outputs, freeze a versioned convention file covering information cutoff, quote session, currency, horizon, entry price, units, fiscal periods, dividends, expenses, execution costs, reinvestment and tax assumptions. Retain original economic assumptions while recomputing mechanics under the common contract. A different assumed growth rate or exit multiple is not an arithmetic error. Classify factual error, missing evidence, stale input, definition mismatch, unsupported assumption and legitimate preference separately; quantify the decision effect when possible.

Keep GAAP, issuer-adjusted, analyst-normalized, TTM, fiscal-year forecast and annualized-quarter earnings distinct. Reconstruct the period bridge and identify every adjustment. A quarter annualization is not normalized TTM; a shorter or longer quarter and cash-tax timing can distort run rates. Rebase only formulas that actually depend on the changed anchor; do not mechanically rewrite a separately authored future earnings target.

## Return attribution and claimholders

For a positive comparable earnings base and multiple, decompose price return as `(1 + earnings growth) * (1 + multiple change) - 1`. Report earnings, multiple and interaction terms, then add distributions and subtract costs/taxes under the comparison contract. Show a constant-multiple control, a separately labeled recovery case, live-entry sensitivity and the exit multiple needed to match the benchmark. If earnings are nonpositive or bases differ, explain why this decomposition is inapplicable.

Connect revenue, operating margins, cash taxes, economic reinvestment, leases, guarantees, acquisition funding and common-share count. Bridge operating earnings to common earnings, including interest, nonoperating items, preferred claims and minority interests. EPS growth does not prove distributable cash growth. Reconcile face, carrying and market debt as different measures; match the chosen claim bridge to the valuation basis. Distinguish unrestricted, restricted and customer/clearing cash. Do not deduct financing principal in equity cash flow and again subtract the same liability without a consistent financing model. Do not count debt-funded buybacks or acquisition growth as free shareholder value.

FCFF uses WACC and an enterprise-to-equity bridge; FCFE uses cost of equity with an explicit financing/share path. A pre-principal cash proxy is not fully distributable FCFE. Choose a consistent treatment of stock compensation: cash replacement, dilution or another explicit economic adjustment, with no double penalty or free dilution offset. Keep future price scenarios separate from present intrinsic value. Separate diagnostic cash models cannot be averaged into corroborating fair values without reconciling their cash and earnings assumptions.

For partial fiscal years, forecast the actual remaining cash flow and its timing; shortening discount exponents on a full-year amount does not fix the stub. Preserve precision until reporting. For steady-state enterprise valuation, link terminal growth to reinvestment and marginal return on invested capital where applicable: reinvestment rate `g / ROIC`, and terminal FCFF `NOPAT * (1 - g / ROIC)`. Distinguish required return from ROIC; test convergence, replacement investment, capital intensity, terminal share and sustainable margins. Equity and sector-specific models require their own coherent capital/payout bridge, not an automatic transplant of the enterprise formula.

Use a fade of marginal ROIC toward the required return as a control, not a universal forecast. With first stable-year NOPAT fixed and marginal ROIC equal to WACC, the stable enterprise value simplifies to `NOPAT / WACC`; extra perpetual growth creates no excess-return value in that setup. Model replacement cohorts and utilization where capital assets drive the result. A high terminal-value share identifies dependence, but alone does not invalidate a long-lived business.

## ETFs and equal evidence requirements

Resolve index, holdings date, share-class aggregation, sector taxonomy and fee basis. Add direct and indirect security exposures across funds; fund labels do not establish diversification or protection from a theme. Use matched constituent earnings, negative-earner rules and aggregation definitions before comparing fund P/E ratios. Price divided by a portfolio P/E is a synthetic proxy, not audited per-share earnings or a holdings-level DCF.

Use a same-index control: identical underlying economics and costs must produce identical modeled returns. Any residual must be traceable to a documented wrapper difference, not mismatched denominators or dates. Apply the same evidence standard to stock and fund cheapness claims. Diversification can justify a portfolio preference under uncertainty without proving intrinsic-value advantage. Broaden the candidate screen when the original universe constrains the answer; label incomplete underwriting rather than declaring an omitted candidate inferior.

## Taxes, cash instruments and implementation

For illustrative taxable returns, net capital gains and losses under the applicable tax-year and character rules before applying rates. Do not tax each winner independently while ignoring an offsetting loss in the same modeled netting group. Include a mixed-sign test because synchronized all-bear/all-base cases can hide this error. Separate dividends, capital results and fees; actual loss utilization, carryovers, dividend qualification, jurisdiction and rates need current user evidence. Never automatically net separate portfolios across different tax years.

Distinguish mark-to-market wealth, continuing after-tax wealth and after-tax liquidation proceeds. If distributions are reinvested, track their tax treatment and cost basis so terminal tax does not tax the same basis again. Turnover is not itself a tax bill; historical mutual-fund tax evidence does not establish the tax drag of a current ETF or the user's tax rate.

SEC yield is standardized historical income net of fund expenses, not promised future distributions. Do not deduct the expense ratio twice or add dividends on top of already modeled yield carry. State whether a full spread represents half at entry plus half at exit. Separate carry, endpoint rate shocks and reset/reinvestment paths; bills, floating-rate notes and long-duration funds have different mechanics. A first-order duration approximation is a scenario, not an exact price forecast. Compare tiny modeled advantages with spreads, fill uncertainty and plausible rate paths before ranking cash alternatives.

Track ex-date entitlement, receivable, payment date, trade date, settlement and withdrawable cash separately. A calendar horizon may end on a closed market day. Verify the actual calendar and broker implementation; sale before a deadline does not imply settled, withdrawable cash by that deadline. Do not invent a binding spending need or arbitrary loss limit when the user supplied neither.

## Evidence gates and review receipt

For a substantial multi-workflow comparison, preserve independent first passes before any peer exposure, self-review each, then have every workflow inspect all peer outputs. Follow with fresh source, arithmetic and omitted-universe checks, a methodological or academic challenge, and separate revised decisions before orchestration. Keep outputs and disagreements identifiable. Use actual separate MCP services when configured; otherwise state which skills, agents and tools actually ran. Scale this process to the task rather than forcing a full council onto a simple factual lookup.

Carry blocking findings forward with stable identifiers and explicit ticker or global scope. A later summary cannot silently drop a blocker or count an unsupported resolution as verified. Keep withheld recommendations out of the released decision view; raw model drafts belong in a labeled audit record. Check persisted artifact bytes when generating integrity hashes, and distinguish successful software tests from truthful source interpretation.

Missing essential cash-flow, ownership or financing evidence blocks a verified fair-value claim. It does not automatically prevent a bounded scenario comparison or an explicitly uncertain allocation preference. Keep the strongest opposing case, opportunity cost and concrete reversal evidence. Do not force consensus across reasonable assumptions.

Describe reviewers as workflows using the actual tools and sources available. Shared underlying models, reused scenarios and syndicated sources are not independent forecasting validations. Numerical tests establish the properties tested, not economic truth. Skill amendments, synthetic fixtures, paper exercises, citations and successful packaging do not train a model or establish improved accuracy, calibrated probabilities, maximum ROI or an optimal market-wide allocation. Such claims require a declared target, information cutoff, frozen predictions, appropriate untouched outcomes and benchmark evaluation at the requested horizon. Report absent evidence plainly.

Treat academic results as methods and bounded historical evidence. Check the exact paper edition, sample, target, horizon, costs, identification and limitations before transferring a finding. Lifetime random-stock outcomes do not estimate a chosen stock's near-term failure probability. Diversified momentum or factor portfolios do not validate a single-stock oversold signal. Historical earnings persistence, macro timing and publication decay warrant controls; they are not universal return haircuts or proof that predictability is impossible. Educational articles clarify definitions without establishing alpha.

For a genuine binary probability forecast, predefine the event and use an appropriate proper score such as Brier loss. For a full distribution use CRPS or another justified proper score; for intervals report a proper interval score with coverage and width. Declare the score orientation. Three unweighted scenarios are not a probability distribution or quantiles. Freeze the universe, origin, version, benchmark and all materially tried specifications; retain rejected candidates and never call a reused tuning set untouched. Account for overlapping horizons and serial dependence without inventing independent sample size. Backtest-overfitting or multiple-testing statistics require their underlying histories and valid tests; they cannot be computed from narrative scenario reports or borrowed as universal confidence thresholds.

The audit receipt should identify the original inputs, source versions, convention file, changed assumptions, calculation checks, remaining gaps, and whether host behavior or future outcomes were actually tested. Retain superseded research as immutable evidence and write corrections in new artifacts.
